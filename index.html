Our heritage

Born of the sea, kept by hand

Long before the towers of West Bay, Qatar's fortune came from the sea. Divers left on dhows each summer, holding their breath for the pearls hidden in Gulf oysters.

Past Times Gems carries that patience forward. Every pearl, stone and setting is chosen by eye, described honestly, and handed over in person here in Doha.
